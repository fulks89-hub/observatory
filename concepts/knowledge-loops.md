# TL;DR

A knowledge loop compounds understanding: capture → understand → atomize → connect → synthesize → question → research → update.

# How it works

“Brain this” is one pass through the loop. Maintenance agents may discover duplicates, missing links, stale claims, and recurring themes, but proposed revisions remain reviewable.
